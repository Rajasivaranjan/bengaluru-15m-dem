Rainfall simulation: rain falls evenly on every 30 m cell for 3 h. Soil and vegetation absorb part of it (SCS Curve Number by ESA land cover), built-up areas lose 20 mm/h to stormwater drains, and the rest flows over the GEM-15 bare-earth surface (2D local-inertial shallow-water model). Pre-computed for six storms. Screening only: drain network, culverts and real rainfall patterns are not modelled.

Rise above drains & lakes floods ground less than this height above the stream or lake it drains to (HAND, Height Above Nearest Drainage, streams ≥ 1 km² catchment). One city-wide level fills every connected area below a single height above sea level. This is a terrain-only bathtub model for screening, not a hydrological flood forecast.
